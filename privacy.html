Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information. The app does not collect or share personal data through its own functionality.

Any amounts entered by users are used only to calculate Zakat. Please do not enter sensitive personal or financial information.

The app does not transmit entered amounts to our servers. Any locally stored information, if applicable, remains on the user's device.

The app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

This app is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.
